# Thermal_Stand
Multiple stands, and holders for my thermal printer

# Why did i made this? 
So i have a thermal printer which i got from print legion and when i ship part i face a really good issue which is when i use my thermal printer my thermal paper always get gluped up and i end up being with a thermal paper with bad quality or just with a lots of wrinkles so yea i really needed a solution for that so i made a practical stand for my thermal print which i can use for my printer which can also hold thermal paper, which saves my thermal paper from getting wrinkles and keeping my paper in one place!! i have also made holders for my thermal paper and all so yea!! 

# What was the hardest part?
Taking measurements!! i had printed the holder and it doesn't fit so like yea i just cooked a lil with measurement so i had to fix that too so i fixed that and then i had made mistakes on some other holders too so i had to work on those measurements too!  
that's why i think the hardest part was this! 

# Features of different parts
# 1st Part:-
This part can hold your printer and your thermal that you are using with the printer it has a small cylinder that can go inside your thermal paper roll and makes it easy to carry cause the thermal paper holder and thermal printer have same frame! 

# 2nd Part:-
This part have is a thermal paper holder which can hold your thermal paper, it also has screw holes through which you can screw it in the wall! it helps to hold your thermal paper! 

# 3rd Part:-
This part can hold one roll of your thermal paper another deck of thermal paper there are two types of thermal paper available  in the market so this can hold both types of paper! 

# 4th Part:-
This part is to just hold your two thermal paper roll you can also use this to hold your other packing materials like tape and packing paper!
